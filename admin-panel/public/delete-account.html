Account & Data Deletion Request

Google Play & Apple App Store User Data Policy Compliance

1. In-App Deletion (Immediate)

If you have the Bocardo app installed:

  1. Open the app (Bocardo Customer, Bocardo Partner, or Bocardo Rider).
  2. Navigate to the Account or Profile screen.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm the action. Your personal profile, contact information, device tokens, and addresses will be deleted immediately.

2. Online Request (Web Submission)

If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your device, submit your deletion request via email to:

Email: privacy@bocardo.app

Please include your registered mobile number (+91) and the Bocardo app you used (Customer, Partner, or Rider). Our support team will verify your identity via SMS/phone and execute data deletion within 7 business days.

3. Data Deletion vs. Retention Summary

Data Type Action Reason
Profile, Name, Phone, Email, Avatar Permanently Deleted Removed from operational databases.
Saved Addresses & GPS coordinates Permanently Deleted Address entries removed.
FCM Push Notification Device Tokens Permanently Deleted All notifications stopped.
Past Completed Order Tax Invoices Archived / Personal Info Anonymised Retained strictly for statutory tax, financial audit, and legal accounting compliance.
