Bocardo Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026 · Last Updated: November 9, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Bocardo Technologies ("Bocardo", "we", "us") collects, uses, and safeguards personal information across our applications: the Bocardo customer app, Bocardo Partner restaurant app, and Bocardo Rider delivery app.

2. Information We Collect

  • Authentication Data: Mobile phone number used for SMS One-Time Password (OTP) verification.
  • Location Information: Foreground location for ordering and restaurant mapping.
  • Background Location (Bocardo Rider Only): Bocardo Rider collects location data in the background even when the app is closed or not in use to enable delivery dispatch, routing, and real-time transit tracking for customers while a partner is on duty.
  • Camera & Photos: Used exclusively when initiated by the user for proof-of-delivery photos or uploading KYC verification documents.
  • Payment Details: Processed directly via secure PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways (Razorpay). We do not store card credentials.
  • Device Identifiers: Firebase push notification tokens and Firebase App Check tokens for abuse prevention.

3. How We Use Data

To fulfill food orders, match delivery partners, facilitate payment transactions, verify partner KYC credentials, prevent fraud, and provide customer support.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted infrastructure providers: Supabase (database & auth), Firebase (push notifications & App Check), Razorpay (payments), and Mapbox / Google Maps (mapping & routing). We never sell your personal data.

5. Account & Data Deletion

How to Delete Your Account:

Users may delete their account in-app under the Account or Profile tab by tapping Delete account, or by submitting an online deletion request at https://bocardo.app/delete-account.

All personal profile details, contact numbers, device tokens, and addresses will be permanently deleted. Statutory tax and ledger records are retained as required by law.

6. Contact & Grievance Officer

For questions or privacy concerns, contact our Grievance Desk at privacy@bocardo.app or support@bocardo.app.
